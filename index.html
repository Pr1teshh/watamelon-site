— 07 / FAQ

Frequently asked.

Which platform should I choose?

Shopify for serious e-commerce, Wix if you want to edit easily yourself, and custom code for speed and zero platform fees. We'll recommend the right fit for your business.

How long does a website take?

Landing pages take 2–3 days. Full websites take 2–3 weeks, depending on pages and content.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. Shopify and Wix builds come with a handover walkthrough, and custom builds come with clean, documented code.

Can you do design only, without building?

Yes. Website Design & UI/UX can stop at Figma — wireframes, UI and clickable prototypes, handed over as dev-ready files for your team.

Do you write the content?

We structure the copy and CTAs for every page, and can write it fully with AI-assisted copywriting.

Do you still make ads and UGC videos?

Yes — as an add-on or a monthly retainer, usually paired with a website or landing page so the traffic has somewhere good to land.

Got an idea brewing?

Tell us what you're thinking — we'll help you squeeze the best out of it.

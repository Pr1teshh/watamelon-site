    — How we score

    Three scores. 34 checks.

    40% of overall

    Conversion

    Does the page turn visitors into enquiries or sales? We look for one clear headline, action-led buttons near the top and always in reach, several ways to get in touch, social proof from real named people, a short lead form, visible pricing, an FAQ, link previews and rich Google results, trust basics and analytics to measure what works.

    35% of overall

    User-friendly

    Is it easy and fast to use, especially on a phone? We check mobile readiness, server speed, page weight, script load, image descriptions, lazy-loaded images, heading structure, navigation, page title, labelled form fields, enough helpful content, language and favicon.

    25% of overall

    Creativity

    Does it feel crafted rather than default? We look at distinctive typography, motion and interaction, rich visuals, a considered colour palette, modern layout techniques and whether it's more than a stock template.

    This is an automated scan of your homepage's code: a quick, honest signal rather than a full audit. The bar is set high on purpose: most websites land between 50 and 80, and passing 85 is rare. Taste, copy and offer can't be measured by a machine, which is exactly why we still do human reviews.
